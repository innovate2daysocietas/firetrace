ASSESSMENT RULES

What a heat signal can tell us.

Detected

An instrument observed a thermal anomaly at a reported coordinate and acquisition time.

Estimated

Some records include FRP, an estimate of fire radiative power expressed in megawatts.

Not established

Ignition cause, exact land destroyed, smoke concentration, injuries and confirmed exposure.
