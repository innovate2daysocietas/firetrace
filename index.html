THE UGANDA QUESTION

Where is burning happening — and what do we actually know?

In agricultural and savanna regions, a satellite heat signal could reflect vegetation burning, crop-residue fires, or another sufficiently hot source. FireTrace helps people ask better questions before drawing conclusions.
